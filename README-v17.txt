A-1 FieldOps Mobile v17 — Social Lead Hub
- Adds authenticated /api/social-lead intake endpoint for normalized social inquiries.
- Endpoint requires SOCIAL_LEAD_WEBHOOK_SECRET and SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY server-side.
- Supports source labeling for Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, WhatsApp and other social inboxes.
- Adds source badges and source filters to Lead Inbox.
- Does not scrape or impersonate social accounts. Direct-message ingestion requires an authorized provider connection/webhook.
