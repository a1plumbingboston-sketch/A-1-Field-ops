SMS Terms

A-1 uses business text messaging for customer service: answering your plumbing and heating questions, discussing a requested quote or appointment, and sending payment receipts when you separately request them.

  • You start the conversation. When you text our business number with a service question, you request replies about that inquiry. This does not enroll you in marketing texts or unrelated future updates.
  • Receipts are a separate choice. You may separately ask A-1 for text payment receipts. Our team explains the texting terms and records your permission for your saved phone number. Providing a phone number, booking work, or submitting the website service form alone does not authorize text receipts.
  • Frequency varies with your inquiries and requested receipts. Message and data rates may apply. Consent to text messages is not a condition of purchasing service.
  • To stop texts, reply STOP. The messaging provider may send one opt-out confirmation. You can contact us by phone or email afterward. Replying START can remove the provider's text-message block; it does not subscribe you to marketing or automatically select text receipts.
  • For help, reply HELP or contact us below. Delivery is not guaranteed, and carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

How we ask for text receipt permission

Our team asks: “May A1 Plumbing & Heating Inc. send payment receipts by text to your saved phone number when email is unavailable? Message frequency varies with payments; message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop or HELP for help. Texting is optional and not required to purchase service. Terms and privacy are available at a-1-field-ops.vercel.app/texting.”

We record your answer, phone number and the date. You may decline or withdraw permission at any time.

Privacy Policy

To manage conversations and requested receipts, we process your phone number, message content, attachments you send, communication preferences, message timestamps, and delivery information. We may associate your conversation with your customer, job, quote, invoice, or payment record.

We use these details to respond to you, provide requested service communications, keep business records, and honor communication preferences. Please do not text card numbers, passwords, access codes, government identifiers, or other sensitive information.

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. We do not share mobile information with affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. Text-messaging opt-in data and consent are not shared for marketing. We use service providers, including Twilio and the providers that host FieldOps, to transmit, store, secure, and operate these communications. Information may also be disclosed when required by law.

Messages and associated records are retained as needed for customer service, business recordkeeping, security, and legal obligations. Contact us to request access, correction, or deletion. Some records, including an opt-out record, may need to be retained to honor your preferences or meet recordkeeping obligations.

This notice covers A-1 customer-service texting and text receipts. Your mobile carrier and any separately linked service have their own privacy practices.
